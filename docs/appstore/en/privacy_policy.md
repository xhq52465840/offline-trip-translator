# Privacy Policy (draft — confirm before release)

Effective date: ____ Developer: ____ (contact: ____)

**In one sentence**: translation and understanding happen entirely offline on your iPhone; we do not collect or upload your conversations, voice or location.

1. **Conversations and voice**: text you type and speech you record are processed only on your device (on-device model and Apple's on-device speech recognition). Nothing is sent to us or any third-party server. Conversation history is stored only on this device and is deleted with the app or via "Clear conversations on this phone".
2. **Network requests the app makes (only these, none contain personal content)**
   - Downloading the offline model (once, about 1 GB, from this project's GitHub Releases or our CDN; GitHub may log IP addresses as usual);
   - Downloading system speech and translation language assets (provided by Apple);
   - Fetching public exchange rates from `api.frankfurter.dev` (ECB reference rates): only USD/IDR/CNY numbers, no conversation content or identifiers. The provider may log IP addresses as usual; see its policy.
   - Optional anonymous usage counts (counts and timings only, never text, translations, recordings or clipboard content); can be turned off in Settings.
3. **Purchases**: handled by Apple In-App Purchase; we never receive payment details. Trial start time and purchase state are stored only on device (Keychain).
4. **What we don't do**: no accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, no cross-app tracking.
5. **Children**: not directed at children under 13; we collect no information from them.
6. **Changes**: updates are announced in the app and on this page.
